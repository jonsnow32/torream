Torream is a high-performance video and media player built on the MPV engine — the same engine trusted by power users worldwide for its unmatched format support and playback quality.

UNIVERSAL FORMAT SUPPORT
Play virtually any video or audio format without conversion: MKV, MP4, AVI, MOV, FLV, WebM, TS, M2TS, and hundreds more. Torream handles what other players can't.

ADVANCED SUBTITLE SUPPORT
Load and sync subtitles from any source. Supports SRT, ASS, SSA, VTT, and more. Fine-tune timing, style, and positioning to match your preferences.

STREAMING & NETWORK PLAYBACK
Stream directly from URLs, HLS/DASH streams, and network shares. Torream fetches and plays remote content with the same speed and reliability as local files.

TORRENT STREAMING
Stream torrent content before the download completes — no waiting for the full file. Built-in torrent engine gets you watching in seconds.

ANDROID TV & FIRE TV READY
Full DPAD remote control support with an optimized TV interface. Navigate your library, control playback, and manage subtitles entirely with your remote.

PICTURE-IN-PICTURE
Continue watching while multitasking with PiP mode. Torream keeps your video visible across all your Android apps.

CHROMECAST SUPPORT
Cast any video to your Chromecast — including local files from your device. Control playback with a full cast player UI: seek, play/pause, skip, and subtitle selection, all without leaving the app.

BACKGROUND AUDIO PLAYBACK
Keep audio running when you switch apps or lock your screen. Full media session integration with lock screen controls and notification controls.

DOWNLOAD FOR OFFLINE VIEWING
Download HTTP, HLS, and torrent content directly to your device for offline playback anywhere.

EQUALIZER
Shape your audio with a built-in 5-band equalizer. Dial in bass, mids, and treble to match your headphones, speakers, or personal taste.

GESTURE CONTROLS
Swipe to adjust brightness, volume, and seek position with intuitive touch gestures — designed for one-handed use.

PHONE, TABLET & TV — ONE APP
Adaptive layout that looks great on every screen size. Bottom navigation on phones, side nav rail on tablets and TV.
