Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2024

Torream ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use the Torream Android application.

Information We Do Not Collect

Torream does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information. Specifically, we do not collect:

  • Your name, email address, or account information
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Location data
  • Media files or content you play or download
  • Browsing or usage history sent to our servers

Data Stored Locally

The following data is stored locally on your device only and is never transmitted to us:

  • Playback history and watch progress
  • Downloaded files and torrent sessions
  • Playlists and favorites
  • App preferences and settings

Third-Party Services

Torream may include third-party advertising SDKs (Google AdMob, AppLovin, Unity Ads, Vungle) that may collect certain data to serve ads. These services have their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob Privacy Policy
  • AppLovin Privacy Policy
  • Unity Ads Privacy Policy
  • Vungle Privacy Policy

Network Requests

Torream makes network requests only to URLs and streams that you explicitly provide or open. The app does not make background connections to our servers. DNS-over-HTTPS (DoH) may be used to resolve domain names securely.

Permissions

Torream requests the following Android permissions:

  • Internet — to stream and download media from URLs you provide
  • Read/Write Storage — to access and save media files
  • Notifications — to show download progress
  • Foreground Service — for background downloads and audio playback

Children's Privacy

Torream is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: support@streamless.cloud
