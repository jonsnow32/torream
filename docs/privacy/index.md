# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 26/08/2025_

## Introduction
This Privacy Policy explains how our application (“the App”, “we”, “our”, or “us”) collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our services.  
Our App is a video player that allows users to play local and online media content. We do not require user registration or login to use the App.

We are committed to ensuring that your privacy is protected and handled in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.

---

## Information We Collect
The App itself does not collect personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number. However, we use **Firebase services** (a Google product) which may automatically collect certain types of data:

- **Device Information & Identifiers**  
  - Examples: Android Advertising ID, Firebase Installation ID.  
  - Purpose: Analytics, app functionality (notifications).

- **App Activity Data**  
  - Examples: Screen views, in-app interactions.  
  - Purpose: Analytics to improve user experience.  

- **Crash Logs & Performance Data**  
  - Examples: Crash reports, error logs, app performance metrics.  
  - Purpose: Identify and fix issues, improve app stability.  

We do not collect sensitive information (such as contacts, photos, or location) unless explicitly stated in future updates.

---

## Data Sharing
- We **do not sell or rent** your personal information to third parties.  
- Data collected through Firebase may be shared with Google in aggregated or anonymized form, in compliance with their [Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).  

---

## Data Usage Purposes
The data collected is used for the following purposes:
- **App Functionality** – Enable push notifications and other essential features.  
- **Analytics** – Understand how users interact with the App to improve user experience.  
- **Diagnostics** – Identify crashes, fix bugs, and optimize performance.  

---

## Data Security
- All data transmitted between the App and Firebase servers is **encrypted in transit** using HTTPS.  
- We do not store user data on our own servers.  

---

## Data Retention & Deletion
- Firebase retains analytics and crash data for a limited period (in accordance with Google’s retention policies).  
- Users may **request deletion of their data** by contacting us at:  
📧 **csplayerhelper@gmail.com*  

Upon request, we will coordinate with Firebase to remove identifiable user data where applicable.

---

## Accounts & Authentication
- The App **does not support account creation or login**.  
- Users cannot log in with external accounts (e.g., Google, Facebook).  

---

## Children’s Privacy
Our App is not specifically directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe your child has provided us with personal data, please contact us for removal.  

---

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Users will be notified of significant changes by updating the “Last updated” date at the top of this page.
